# Minecraft 3D — Player Guide

This is a quick reference for the current gameplay systems. Some actions depend on having the required item and selecting a compatible version profile.

## Enchanted Grove & Artifact Collection (1.9.9)

- Search newly created 1.9.9 worlds for the rare **Зачарованная роща** biome. It has moss-covered ground, denser trees, scattered glow crystals and ferns.
- The HUD displays the biome name when you enter it. Visiting it completes the new eight-biome exploration goal.
- Open the two **Ancient Caches** in the sandstone temples. One temple guarantees the **Sun Sigil**, the other the **Moon Sigil**; both also contain an **Ancient Relic**.
- Press **M** to review the three artifact milestones and the **Ancient Heritage** achievement for collecting the full set.
- The enchanted biome is generated only in new 1.9.9 worlds. Existing 1.9.8 worlds keep their original terrain and exploration target.

## Ancient Temples & Relics (1.9.8)

- In newly created 1.9.8 worlds, look for two sandstone **Ancient Temples** far from the starting area.
- Each temple has an open arena guarded by a **Temple Warden**. Aim at the guardian and click the left mouse button to attack repeatedly.
- Open the **Ancient Cache** with **F** while aiming at it. Its seed-based reward includes an Ancient Relic and either a Sun Sigil or a Moon Sigil, plus supplies.
- All three artifacts are persistent inventory items and have their own icons. Existing 1.9.7 saves do not receive the new temples automatically.

## Crystal Guardians & Hidden Treasures (1.9.7)

- Look for a **Crystal Grotto** in newly created 1.9.7 worlds; some grottos contain a crystal tree, a stone arena and a **Crystal Cache**.
- The **Crystal Sentinel** guards the arena. Aim at it and click the left mouse button to attack; click again for each next hit. Its remaining health is shown in the combat message.
- Open the Crystal Cache with **F** while aiming at it. It gives a fixed, seed-based bundle of emeralds, metal ingots and cooked fish.
- The sentinel and new structures are not inserted into existing 1.9.6 saves.

## Cave Biomes & Glowflora (1.9.6)

- Explore **Crystal Grotto** rooms for luminous crystals and amethyst-lined walls.
- **Moss Caverns** contain cave moss and small fern clusters; **Underground Lakes** have wider chambers and water pools.
- When you are deep underground, the HUD displays the current cave-biome name.
- Cave biomes are generated in newly created 1.9.6 worlds. Older saves are not regenerated automatically.

## Crystal Caverns & Gem Veins (1.9.5)

- Explore the deeper cave rooms and look for amethyst-lined walls and small underground pools.
- Ruby ore appears in deeper layers; sapphire ore can be found a little higher. Both are rare and need an iron-tier pickaxe.
- The new deposits are generated in newly created 1.9.5 worlds; existing worlds are not regenerated automatically.

## Survival loop

1. Explore the world and gather wood, stone, ores, and food.
2. Use the inventory (**I**) and crafting screen (**C**) to make tools and supplies.
3. Make a hoe and prepare farmland, then plant and harvest wheat.
4. Build a furnace, use fuel and ore to smelt materials, and cook raw fish.
5. Visit villagers to trade selected supplies for emeralds and useful items.
6. Fish near water with a rod, then cook the catch.
7. Open achievements (**M**) to review exploration and gameplay progress.
8. In the 1.1.0 profile, explore taiga and swamp regions, search caves for amethyst veins and underground pools, and meet foxes and turtles. A dog companion follows you around the world.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Hold left mouse button | Mine the targeted block in survival mode (1.9.4+); releasing cancels progress |
| I | Inventory |
| C | Crafting |
| M | Achievements and artifact collection progress |
| T | Switch available tool |
| P | Cast fishing rod while aiming at nearby water |
| F | Interact with villagers or a furnace |
| G | Farming actions / place furnace where applicable |
| E | Eat available food |
| F5 | Switch first-person / third-person camera |
| V | Cycle the four player color palettes |
| F6 | Save world |
| ESC | Pause menu |
| F10 | Exit |

## World saves

World data is stored separately from player inventory and progression sidecar files. Keep the whole save folder together when making backups. Do not delete sidecar files if you want to preserve inventory, farm growth, or achievements.

## Notes

- The world format is documented in the project changelog; the current profile keeps format 2.
- Existing worlds are not automatically regenerated when you switch to a newer profile.
- Farm growth currently advances while the game is open; offline crop growth is not part of the documented 0.9.5 behavior.
- Back up important worlds before trying experimental mods.
